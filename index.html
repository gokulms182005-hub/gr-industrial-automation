About GR Industrial Automation

Engineering reliability into every control system we build.

GR Industrial Automation was founded by a team of control systems engineers who believed manufacturers deserved automation partners who stay accountable long after commissioning. Today we design, build, and support automation systems across ten industries, from single-machine retrofits to full-plant SCADA rollouts.

We combine deep field experience with modern software practice — every panel we ship and every HMI screen we design is documented, tested, and supported by engineers who understand the plant floor.
